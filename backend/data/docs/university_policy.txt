Section 3.2 Attendance Policy
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to qualify for final examinations.
Students below 75% attendance must apply for a condonation through the department head.

Section 4.1 Paid Leave Policy
Full-time staff receive 15 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.
Unused leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

Section 5.3 Scholarship Policy
Merit scholarships are awarded to students with a CGPA of 3.5 or above.
Applications must be submitted before the semester registration deadline.
